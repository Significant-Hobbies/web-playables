# War Chest

Canonical: https://idle.aliveville.com/play/war-chest/

A tactical story game about bringing the valley’s stolen winter wages home. Command Rowan, Lysa and Fen across The Last Crossing, then prepare your company around a physical 6 × 5 equipment chest.

## Play

Press Play to load the game. Read the opening conversation, prepare your company, then choose a companion and an order. End turn when you are ready for enemies to act. The campaign contains twelve encounters and three chapters.

- Click a companion portrait or press 1–5 to select freely.
- Choose an order, then a legal target. T cycles targets; F confirms.
- Space ends the turn. Z undoes the last order in the current turn.
- In camp, pack equipment into the chest. R rotates; WASD moves the cursor; F places.
- Menu contains the manual, sound and reduced-motion settings.

Browser prototype. Best in landscape on a larger screen. Saves remain in this browser; private browsing or clearing site data may remove them. Native saves are separate. The browser toolbar exports a JSON copy for inspection. This build has no import control; clearing browser storage removes playable progress. This Godot game is outside the YouTube Playables lane.
