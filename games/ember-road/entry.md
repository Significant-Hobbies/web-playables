# Ember Road

An original single-player idle battle game. Begin a chapter and your knight walks and fights automatically. At each level-up, choose one of three oaths to shape this journey.

Gold and forged gear survive defeat. Return to the forge to improve your blade, mail or charm, then retry or enter the next unlocked chapter. Five chapters each end with a boss. Oaths reset between journeys.

Use pointer or touch controls. Space pauses during a journey when a button is not focused. Keys 1–3 choose an oath. Settings offers reduced motion. Progress stays in this browser; no account or network is needed.
